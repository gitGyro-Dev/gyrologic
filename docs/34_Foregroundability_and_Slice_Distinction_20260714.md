## Foregroundability remains on the not-yet side

Foregroundability does not yet produce local determination.

```text
foregroundability
≠
Slice
≠
Stability
```

At this stage, one relation or direction only becomes more noticeable, suggestive, or easier to anticipate.

No Gyro path-opening process has yet begun.

## Slice begins local movement from not-yet

Slice is not merely that something looks relevant.

Slice begins when a path is actually opened through Structure toward an establishment.

```text
possible direction
↓
Slice begins
↓
path opening
```

The word `act` is useful as an intuition but too strong as a definition.

Slice may be observational, relational, interpretive, informational, or operational.

Thus:

```text
Slice is active relative to foregroundability
```

but:

```text
Slice
≠
physical action only
```

## Slight visibility is not Stability readability

```text
slightly visible direction
≠
readable continuing establishment
```

A direction may be sensed before its relation, Boundary, Difference, path, or continuability becomes readable.

## Relation to Orientation

A provisional image is:

```text
incorporated readability
↓
pre-Slice directional cue
↓
foregroundability
↓
Operator Orientation
↓
Slice
```

The actual relation may not always be linear.

The safe distinction is:

```text
foregroundability
=
pre-Slice directional visibility
```

```text
Slice
=
actual path-opening process
```

## Not subtraction

Foregroundability does not divide Structure into foreground and background.

```text
Structure - foreground
≠
background
```

Structure is not reduced.

One possible direction only becomes more noticeable under current conditions.

## Examples

### Ocean

`salty` may suggest a Slice toward chemical composition, marine ecology, or water identity.

But `salty` is not itself the Slice.

### Society

`gender equality` may suggest a Slice toward legal, social, institutional, or historical relations.

But the concept is not itself the Slice.

### GyroAuth

Prior authentication and attack patterns may suggest a Slice toward device anomaly, behavioral divergence, trajectory discontinuity, or contextual risk.

But those patterns do not themselves execute authentication.

## Preliminary formal image

Let:

```text
Fg(q ; C_n)
```

mean that readability element `q` can become directionally noticeable under current conditions `C_n`.

This does not imply:

```text
Fg(q ; C_n)
→
Σ_n automatically
```

Foregroundability may contribute to Slice initiation, but it is not Slice.
