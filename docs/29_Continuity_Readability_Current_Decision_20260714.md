# Continuity Readability — Current Decision

## 1. Position

At the current stage, the theory does not attempt to define one universal form of the `meaningful relation` by which local Gyro realizations become readable as continuous.

The relevant relation may differ by:

```text
Structure type
Context
Operator Orientation
Slice
Difference
Boundary
identity criterion
causal or functional domain
```

Therefore, the current common description remains intentionally weak:

```text
A later local realization is readable as continuous with an earlier one
when there is some admissible relation by which the two can be traced.
```

Japanese:

```text
前後の局所的な成立の間に、
何らかの意味ある関係性があり、
その関係を辿ることができるとき、
それらは連続しているものとして読まれ得る。
```

The content of `meaningful relation` is not fixed universally at this stage.

---

## 2. Distinction from Identity

```text
Identity
=
whether changed realizations may be treated as the same entity or Structure
```

```text
Continuity readability
=
whether separated local realizations may be traced through an admissible relation
```

Thus:

```text
Identity
≠
Continuity readability
```

A relation may remain traceable even when Structure identity changes.

Likewise, identity may be asserted while the connecting relation remains unreadable.

---

## 3. Relation to Trajectory

```text
local Gyro realizations
+
traceable relations
+
Orientation-relative tracing
→
readable Trajectory
```

Trajectory is not the relation itself.

It is the trajectory that becomes readable when accumulated and folded traces are followed through selected relations.

---

## 4. Current Scope

The current theory commits only to the following:

```text
continuity is not mere temporal adjacency
continuity is not mere similarity
continuity is not identical to identity
continuity is not created arbitrarily by Operator
continuity requires some traceable relation
```

The exact form of that relation remains domain-relative and is deferred to later mathematical and scientific formalization.
