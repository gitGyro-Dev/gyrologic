# Jxiv Revision Reason — Minimal Formal Model v2 (English)

## Recommended text for Jxiv revision reason

This revision clarifies several theoretical distinctions in the Minimal Formal Model while preserving the invariant Core of Gyro Logic (`Structure → Slice → Stability`). In particular, it clarifies `slice-done` as a local unitization rather than the objective termination of an underlying event or process, and explicitly preserves the distinction between local articulation / local unitization and Stability.

The revision also refines the treatment of readability. The canonical wording of Stability is retained, while `Readable(...)` is no longer treated as a universally defined independent formal predicate; where formal readability notation is used, it is treated as a domain-relative placeholder unless a specialized model supplies justified semantics.

In addition, the revised manuscript clarifies how local boundaries may be supplied or constrained by current Operator judgment, Orientation and Context, inherited protocols or institutional criteria, or strong event-side transitions, without assuming that every boundary is necessarily externally constrained. A minimal anti-post-hoc requirement is added so that such frames or claimed boundary provenance cannot be introduced only after the boundary has been selected without independent support.

The revision further distinguishes retrospective establishment from the past event itself and states that a remaining trace may support a present establishment about a past event without implying that a single trace uniquely determines that event.

Finally, the manuscript clarifies that the term *minimal* is used in an exploratory and operational sense and does not claim a proof of unique, cardinal, or order-theoretic minimality. Figures, comparisons with existing mathematical fields, worked examples, references, and the overall supporting argument are retained.

No change is made to the canonical Core or its order.

## Short version

This revision preserves the Gyro Logic Core (`Structure → Slice → Stability`) while clarifying `slice-done` as local unitization rather than objective event termination, refining the formal status of `Readable(...)` as domain-relative rather than universal, clarifying possible sources and constraints of local boundaries together with an anti-post-hoc provenance requirement, and adding a clearer distinction between past events and present retrospective establishments. The meaning of *minimal* is also clarified as exploratory/operational rather than a proof of strict mathematical minimality. The main figures, examples, mathematical comparisons, references, and supporting argument are retained.
